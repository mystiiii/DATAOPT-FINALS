Sales Performance Report

Revenue breakdown by category, top products, and sales territories for FY2011 to FY2014

Key Takeaways

Bikes brought in 85.2% ($94.6M) of total sales, led by the Mountain-200 line ($4.2M). Components brought in 10.6% ($11.8M), and clothing plus accessories made up the rest.

Annual sales rose from $12.6M in 2011 to $43.9M in 2013, then reached $20.1M in the first half of 2014.

Southwest was the top territory with $24.18M across 5,716 orders, followed by Northwest at $16.08M. Central was the lowest at $7.92M across 1,399 orders.
