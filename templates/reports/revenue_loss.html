Revenue and Loss Report

Gross revenue, promotional discounts, and manufacturing scrap for FY2011 to FY2014

Key Takeaways

Gross order volume reached $110,373,889. After promotional discounts, net revenue was $109,846,381.

Promotional discounts totaled $527,508, which is 0.48% of gross revenue, having very little effect on margins.

Production scrap cost $412,390 across 11,300 discarded units. Most of the scrap came from tooling temperature drops (3,480 units) and handling damage (2,150 units).
