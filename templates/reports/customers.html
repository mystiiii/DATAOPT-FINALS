Consultant Analytical Interpretation

1. Top High-Value Customers: The top 5 spending accounts are all B2B Store Retailers led by Better Large Sporting Goods ($989,184 across 12 orders) and Metropolitan Bicycle Supply ($961,675), averaging ~$80K per bulk order.

2. Store vs Individual Comparison: Commercial B2B stores represent only 3.6% of accounts (701 stores), yet generate 76% of total revenue ($93.9M). In contrast, 18,484 individual retail consumers contribute 24% ($29.3M) with an average order of $1,060.

3. Geographic Concentration: Customer spending is concentrated in the western United States (California $19.4M, Washington $14.2M, Texas $9.6M), with significant international clusters in Australia ($9.1M) and the UK ($8.5M).
