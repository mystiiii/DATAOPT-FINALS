Cost and Margin Analysis

Manufacturing costs, list prices, margins, and vendor procurement spend for FY2011 to FY2014

Key Takeaways

Accessories have the highest margins at roughly 62.5% to 62.7% (such as water bottles and tire tubes). Assembled bikes carry lower margins, with Mountain-200 at 45.4% and Road-150 at 39.3%.

Between 2012 and 2013, production costs for the Mountain-200 fell from $1,304.60 to $1,251.98, improving margin by 4.0%.

Purchasing spent $53.2M across 4,012 purchase orders. Most spend went to Superior Bicycles ($8.42M over 182 orders) and Professional Athletic Consultants ($7.14M).
