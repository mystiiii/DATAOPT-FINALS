Revenue and Loss Report

Unifying gross revenue, sales promotional discounts, and production scrap waste (FY2011–FY2014)

Consultant Analytical Interpretation

1. Gross vs Net Revenue: Total gross order volume is $110,373,889. Deducting contractual promotional discounts yields a net realized revenue of $109,846,381.

2. Discount Loss Impact: Promotional volume discounts represent -$527,508 (0.48%) of total gross revenue. This indicates that discounting has minimal negative drag on gross margins and does not threaten operational solvency.

3. Production Scrap Waste: Manufacturing operations incurred $412,390 in material scrap across 11,300 scrapped units. Scrap is concentrated in tooling temperature drops (3,480 units) and handling damage (2,150 units), requiring preventative maintenance rather than product redesign.
